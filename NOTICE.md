# NOTICE

## PRS-001: Provably Rare Standard

This repository establishes the Genesis Draft of **PRS-001: Provably Rare Standard**, a DigitalOwnership Proof Standard initially authored by **Brandon Wells**.

PRS-001 defines a cryptographic rarity and provenance framework for digital assets, including NFTs, artworks, model outputs, datasets, documents, media files, and other digital artifacts.

The standard describes how assets may be anchored to SHA-256 entropy hashes, assigned mathematically verifiable leading-zero rarity classes, committed into public proof structures, and verified through reproducible procedures.
